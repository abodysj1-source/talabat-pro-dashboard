Recommended path — C, with D as the growth track

C · Structured win-winTrim the Pro fee 4 → 3 AED and add a 0.5-pt commission rebate — both growth-linked, reviewed quarterly, reversible. Targets the exact lever that caused the pain (the Pro fee), not the whole commission book.
D · Grow AOVBundles, upsell and a higher minimum lift the bill toward AED 60 — the unchanged 4 AED fee shrinks from ~8.7% to ~6.7% of each bill and profit rises with no concession at all.
Never open with BA blanket commission cut discounts 100% of orders to fix a ~48% problem, is permanent, and sets precedent across every key account. Last-resort fallback only.

How to run the conversation

1Reframe to total profit — profit is up ~AED 365k/mo (+20%); the rising % hid a winning year.
2Validate with their data — ask for Pro vs non-Pro AOV, frequency and retention cohorts to replace assumptions with facts.
3Show the floor — walk scenario A live; let them move the defection slider themselves.
4Offer C, pilot for one quarter — growth-linked, with agreed KPIs (orders, AOV, Pro share, profit/order).
5Open the D track in parallel — menu engineering and bundles cost talabat nothing and help most.
