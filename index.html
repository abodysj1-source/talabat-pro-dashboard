Click a scenario to load its settings into the sliders, or move the sliders yourself. Everything updates and explains itself. Illustrative monthly model, all figures AED.
The story in one line: this account now gets ~48% of orders from talabat Pro (was 12%). They pay talabat a 4 AED fee on each Pro order over 30 AED, so their total payment to talabat grew. They want to drop Pro or cut commission — but the 30 AED minimum lifts basket sizes and brings loyal repeat customers, so their actual profit went up. Marketing spend now earns its own orders through an adjustable ROI. Below: by how much, why dropping Pro costs them market share, a win-win deal (C), and a grow-the-basket play (D).
💡 Why the 4 AED fee isn’t just a cost: to skip the delivery charge a Pro customer must spend over 30 AED — so they add items (bigger basket) — and Pro members come back more often as loyal, high-value regulars. That fee is buying higher order values and repeat customers.
⚠️ Dropping Pro is a market-share decision, not just an order count: those loyal Pro customers don’t disappear — they move to competitors who offer Pro. The brand loses visibility on the app and the lifetime value of its best regulars. The profit drop shown for “Drop Pro” is the floor; the real cost is ceding share to rivals.
